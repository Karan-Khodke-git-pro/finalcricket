How It Started

A Passion That Turned Into a Purpose

The story begins at the age of 8, when cricket first became more than just a game. By 9, a spot on the school's Under-9 team confirmed it — there was no looking back from here. At 12, a move into tennis-ball cricket set the course for everything that followed, bringing with it years of tournaments, championships, and trophies.

Being a cricketer meant seeing the game from the other side too — watching talented tennis cricketers hold back because branded bats were priced out of reach. So in 2022, that lifelong passion became a profession: the first batch of just 10 tennis cricket bats, made by hand. Today, thousands of bats later, the same idea still drives every bat we make — quality that performs, priced so any cricketer can own one.
