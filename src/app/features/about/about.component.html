Our Story

Born from a Passion for the Perfect Bat

SG Cricket was founded in 1978 by master craftsman Surendra Gupta in Meerut, India — the heartland of cricket bat manufacturing. What started as a small workshop of 5 artisans has grown into a world-class manufacturing facility producing over 50,000 bats annually.

Our philosophy has remained unchanged: every bat must deliver the perfect combination of power, balance, and feel. We use Grade 1+ English Willow and hand-select Kashmir Willow to ensure only the finest wood becomes an SG bat.
